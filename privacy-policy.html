Landjack Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Landjack (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a Florida-focused commercial real estate research app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the app handles, how that information is used, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • Landjack is local-first: your projects, notes, and research threads are stored on your device.
  • We do not operate user accounts, sell personal information, show ads, or use third-party tracking SDKs.
  • When you research a property, the app sends queries you initiate (such as an address, parcel ID, or map coordinates) to public records and licensed data providers so results can be shown to you.
  • Location is used only when you choose to center the map on yourself or run a location-based lookup.

Information stored on your device

Landjack may store the following locally on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Projects, addresses, parcel identifiers, and research conversation history
  • Underwriting inputs, notes, and exported PDFs you create
  • App preferences and cached lookup results to improve performance
  • Optional API keys or provider settings you enter in Settings (if any)

This on-device data is not uploaded to a Landjack user account because Landjack does not provide sign-in or cloud sync in the current release.

Information sent over the network

When you search, open a project, or request research, Landjack transmits only what is needed to fulfill that request. Depending on the feature you use, this may include:

  • Street addresses, RE numbers, folio numbers, or other parcel identifiers
  • Latitude and longitude derived from map position, geocoding, or parcel geometry
  • Approximate device location, only if you tap “locate me” or otherwise initiate a location-based action

These queries are sent to Landjack’s backend and/or third-party services listed below. We do not use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Third-party services

Landjack displays and processes data from outside sources so you can research parcels. Providers may include:

  • Landjack API (api.landjack.com) — proxies selected licensed lookups (for example market listings, HUD data, and Florida entity records) so provider secrets are not embedded in the app
  • County and state GIS / property appraiser sources — Florida public parcel, ownership, assessed value, land use, and zoning records
  • FEMA — flood hazard zone information
  • HUD and related federal open data — fair market rents, income limits, opportunity zones, and similar housing datasets
  • Florida Sunbiz — public business entity and officer records when entity ownership is researched
  • Market rate data feeds — public treasury, SOFR, mortgage, and related financial indicators shown in the app
  • Apple MapKit and geocoding — maps, search suggestions, and address geocoding
  • Google Street View Static API — optional property imagery when configured
  • Zillapi and other licensed property-data APIs — market listings, comps, tax history, and related property-market fields when available

Each third party processes information according to its own terms and privacy practices. Landjack requests only the minimum data needed to return the research you asked for.

What we do not collect

  • No Landjack account, username, or password
  • No payment card or billing information in the app
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) collection for ad targeting
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the current release
  • No sale of personal information

Permissions

Location (When In Use): Used to center the map on your position or support spatial lookups you start. Landjack does not collect location in the background.

Data retention

Project data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Cached network responses may be stored temporarily on-device to speed up repeat lookups. Server logs maintained for the Landjack API, if any, are used for operations and security and are not used to build advertising profiles.

Children’s privacy

Landjack is a professional research tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke location permission in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete projects, clear app data, or uninstall Landjack at any time.
  • You control when research is run by opening a project or initiating a lookup.

International users

Landjack is designed for Florida parcel research. If you use the app outside the United States, your queries may still be processed by U.S.-based service providers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when updates are posted. Continued use of Landjack after an update means you accept the revised policy.
